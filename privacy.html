Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Overview

This website and the personal applications associated with it (including command-line tools that access Google services such as Google Drive and Gmail on behalf of the account owner) are personal, self-hosted utilities used solely by their owner.

Data we collect

The associated applications access Google account data (Drive files, Gmail, Docs, Sheets) only on behalf of the authenticated owner, via OAuth 2.0. No data is collected, sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's own machines and can be revoked at any time from the Google account security settings.

Data storage

All data remains in the owner's own Google account and local devices. No external servers process or store user data.
